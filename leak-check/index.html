How we count

  1. Calls that rang out, minus the ones you called back, are the calls nobody returned.

  2. A week times 52, divided by 12, gives a month.

  3. Times the share that would have booked gives the jobs lost.

  4. Times what you keep on a job gives the profit lost.

  5. Quotes and retyping, if you fill them in, are added on top.

Not counted here: reviews , past customers nobody called back and texts in one language ES.
