Your records

Invoices, calendar, job list and call history. You run the exports or give us a user seat of our own, never a shared password. The call history shows when calls came in and whether anyone answered, not what was said.

The sheet

We go over it with you on a call: every problem with a dollar figure and what it would take to fix. If a setting you already pay for fixes one, we tell you.

The sheet is yours to keep, whether or not you hire us for a fix.

What the walk never does

  • Renew, or add a charge. It's one charge.
  • Ask for a password.
  • Change anything in your phone, software or accounts.
  • Text your customers.
  • Put your files into an AI tool unless you say yes. They're deleted once your sheet is delivered.

How we handle your records is on the privacy page.

Full terms

Where the sample numbers come from

$341 is HomeAdvisor's national average plumber visit (2026, cost guide); 44.3% is Level's median job gross margin (vendor data). 24% is the share of calls that trade shops with fewer than 5 technicians booked at all hours (ServiceTitan blog, 2022, vendor data).

On the AC and heating sheet, 1 in 4 comes from ServiceTitan's January 2026 data on commercial service agreements: 29.8% renewed when renewal was manual and 49.1% with auto-renew and email reminders, so about 1 in 4 of the plans that would have lapsed were kept (vendor data). $189 is a sample yearly fee for a plan with two tune-ups, within published Florida plan prices.

On the property manager sheet, 10% is a common maintenance markup in management agreements (BiggerPockets forum, owners and managers reporting; 0 to 20% seen, or a flat fee instead). 70 bills is a sample: about 5% of the 1,400 or so vendor bills an office with 212 rentals pays in a year (about 600 in turn week, plus 3 to 5 work orders a rental), at a sample $180 a bill.

On the pest control sheet, 1 in 2 and 20% are our own sample rates, and your walk puts your own numbers in their place: 1 in 2 lapsed termite renewals won back by a follow-up call, and 20% more declined autopay charges recovered once retries run on a schedule. For scale, Recurly's 2018 study of more than 1,300 subscription businesses found that over 45% of the charges declined for the three most common reasons were recovered (vendor data). $225 is a renewal's $271 average fee across $325 bait and $195 liquid agreements, less a $46 yearly inspection. On the pest control page, $7.50 a foot for trench-and-rod termite treatment sits inside two national cost guides, Fixr's $4 to $14.50 and Polyguard's $3 to $15.

4Step 4: One fix

Paid Only if you want it.

We suggest starting with one fix you can watch move in your own records.

Each fix has its own fixed price, never by the hour, and you hear it with the sheet in hand, before any work starts. Any software it needs is opened in your name, at cost, never marked up.

Want everything fixed at once? We quote that from the sheet too, and the prices add up.

Nothing goes to your customers, or into an AI tool, without your OK.

Everything runs in accounts you own, and every fix is built to fall back to how your business worked before.

What a fix can look like

  • Every supplier bill checked against the job it was bought for.
  • Finished jobs listed each morning until each one is invoiced.
  • The quote sent the day of the visit, with a reminder to you if nobody answers.
  • A job typed once and filled in everywhere else.
  • Emails and attachments read and filed into the right job folder.
  • Reminders to your team and customers, all year.

Questions owners ask

What does it cost?

We publish one price, the walk's, in step 2 above, with its terms. It is paid when booked, and you keep the sheet either way, even if you never hire us for a fix.

Everything after the walk is quoted from your sheet, so that number comes after the walk, not before. Each fix has its own fixed price, never by the hour, and any software fees show on their own line, at cost. You hear each price before any work starts. The first conversation is free.

Do you work outside Gainesville?

We are based in Gainesville and walk businesses across Alachua County. Farther in Florida by arrangement. Every walk includes a visit, so call and we will tell you if the drive works.

What data do you need, and what do you do with it?

Your own records: invoices, quotes, your calendar and job list, and your call history with your written OK. You run the exports or give us a user seat of our own, never a shared password. We keep the files encrypted and delete them once your sheet is delivered. The privacy page has the rest.

Do I have to stop work for the visit?

No. We set the day and time with you when you book, around your busiest hours.

Do you record calls or film anything?

Only with your OK, and Florida law needs everyone on a call to agree before it is recorded. The call history we use shows when calls came in and whether anyone answered, not what was said.

Will this replace my office staff?

It replaces the retyping and the chasing, like the job typed into three places or the quote that waits until Friday. What you do with the time it frees is up to you.

Do I have to switch software?

No, not unless you want to. We start with what you already pay for. If new software would pay for itself, you hear what it costs and what it should bring back, and you decide.

What happens when my business grows or I change software?

Tell us first, and we tell you in writing what it touches and what it would cost. More jobs, trucks or staff on the fixes you have cost nothing extra from us.

Do you sell software?

Only when it pays for itself and you want it. We start with the tools you already have: we set them up and connect them, and when nothing on the market fits how your business works, we build a custom tool for it, often in Python, that runs in your own accounts. If we think new software would serve you better, we tell you what it costs, what it should bring back and why, and you decide. If you'd rather replace your tools, we can do that too and supply the new software, with its fees on their own line, at cost.

Most fixes are a setting, a spreadsheet or a small tool inside what you already use. Where a fix uses AI, it is on your own account and only on what you approve. We take no commissions on the tools we suggest.

Can you text my customers for me?

Your business sends the texts, from your own number, and you approve every word. We set them up to follow Florida's texting rules, and one STOP ends every text. Our article has the rules in plain words.

What if the fix does not work?

Every fix is built to fall back to how your business worked before, and to alert us when it does. While you have the monthly check, fixing what we built is covered.

The monthly check shows what the fix did in your own numbers. If it did not move them, you see that there, and you can cancel any month.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel before the visit starts and before any of your records are shared, and you get the walk fee back. After that, if the sheet wasn't worth it to you, text or email us within 7 days of the call where you go over it, and you get the walk fee back.

You keep the sheet either way. The monthly check runs month to month: cancel by email before the next billing date. If you stop a fix or a build partway, the agreement says what is owed for the work done.

Do you work in Spanish?

Yes. The free call, the walk, your sheet, the monthly check and the one-page handover sheet can all be in Spanish. If your booking tool texts customers only in English, that can be one of the problems we fix.
