1. The free call

Free, up to 20 minutes, by phone

Before we talk, we look your shop up on Google, so you don't spend the call explaining what you do. We bring one number from it, like how many reviews it shows or the hours it lists. If someone else keeps your books, bring them on the call.

“Your listing says you close at five. The sheet will show what happens to the calls after that.”

An example of the one number we bring.

You tell us what eats your week. We tell you plainly whether a walk fits your shop. If you say no, we don't call or text again unless you ask.

2. The walk. Paid when booked. Three parts: the visit, the call data and the sheet.

What the visit looks at

  1. 1The phone · counter

    6:12 PM, after close. Nobody called back.

  2. 2The whiteboard · office

    Visit Tuesday. Quote Friday.

  3. 3The trucks · lot

    Supply ticket in the door pocket. No job number.

  4. 4The invoice pile · desk

    Paper ticket, calendar, QuickBooks. Same job, three times.

Then we follow one job from the first call to the paid invoice and the next visit.

  1. 1Week one

    The visit

    Sized to your shop: 3 to 4 hours where Jobber, Housecall Pro or QuickBooks runs, longer when everything runs off your cell phone.

  2. 2The past 30 days

    The call data

    With your written OK, the call history your phone company already keeps. Where it keeps none, your staff keeps a hand tally by the phone for the next 30 days. It shows when calls came in and whether anyone answered. It is not a recording.

  3. 35 business days

    The sheet

    Within five business days of having the data, on a walkthrough call: every leak with a dollar figure, the formula behind it and the effort to fix it. If a setting you already pay for closes a leak, the sheet says so.

The sheet is yours to keep, whether or not you hire us for a fix.

What we need from you

  • Your written OK for the call history
  • Exports of your quotes and invoices, which you run, or a user seat of our own
  • Your calendar
  • A time for the visit, and one for the walkthrough call

Never a shared password. How we handle your records is on the privacy page.

What the walk never does

  • Renew, or add a charge. It's one charge.
  • Ask for a password.
  • Change anything in your phone, software or accounts.
  • Text your customers.
  • Put your files into an AI tool unless you say yes. They're deleted once your sheet is delivered.

3. One fix

Priced from the sheet

We start with the leak that recovers the most for the effort, one you can watch move in your own records within 90 days.

Each fix is priced on its own, so you can start with one. The price is fixed, never by the hour, and you hear it with the sheet in hand, before any work starts. Any software or service the fix needs is opened in your name, and its fee shows on its own line, at cost, never marked up. You pay half when you sign and half when it's working.

The whole shop

If you would rather fix everything together, we quote it from the sheet too, one fix at a time, and the prices add up. We suggest one fix first, to see it hold.

Built inside the tools you already use. The same fix, three ways.

The fix for row 7, on sample data: a supply bill matcher. It reads the supplier's PDF bill when the email arrives, checks each line against the invoice for the same job and flags the $41.20 relief valve. You choose: add it to the invoice, or no charge. Nothing is sent or billed on its own.

Every fix runs on rules, not guesses, inside accounts you own. If something changes, it stops and tells us. If a fix ever breaks, it falls back to how your shop worked before, and we look at it the next business morning.

4. The monthly check

Sized to the shop

One page, once a month: what the fix did in your numbers, what broke and how we handled it, the next leak with a dollar figure and how many change requests your plan includes.

At 90 days we compare what the fix brought back with what the sheet said. If it brought back less, the monthly check goes down to match, and it does not go up in the first year. Cancel any month, by email. In storm season you can add a same-evening check on the after-hours flow, quoted from the sheet like everything else.

  • Cancel any month
  • Up to 2 small changes a month
  • Fixed from the next business morning
  • Never goes up in the first year
What the monthly check covers, and what it doesn't

Covered

  • Watching every fix we built
  • Fixing what breaks, starting the next business morning
  • Up to 2 small changes a month, each about an hour of work or less
  • The one-page report
  • The next leak, with a dollar figure

Not covered

  • New fixes, each quoted on its own
  • Software subscriptions, in your name and at cost
  • Hardware
  • Outages at other companies' software
  • Marketing or ads
  • Dispatch software

What sets the price after the walk

Everything after the walk is quoted from your sheet, so the number comes after the walk, not before. Three things move it:

How big the leak is , how many trucks (or locations) you run, and what your tools can already do .

That is why there is no price list. Two shops in the same trade can leak different amounts, in different places. The walk is the only price we can name before we see your shop.

What happens on the free call?

Before we talk, we look your shop up on Google, so you don't spend the call explaining what you do. We bring one number from it, like how many reviews it shows or the hours it lists.

You tell us what eats your week. We tell you plainly whether a walk fits. If you say no, we don't call or text again unless you ask.

Do you work outside Gainesville?

We are based in Gainesville and walk shops across Alachua County. Farther in Florida by arrangement. Every walk includes a visit, so call and we will tell you if the drive works.

What data do you need, and what do you do with it?

Three things from your own records: the call history your phone company keeps, your quote and invoice exports and your calendar. We use the call history only with your written OK. You run the exports, or give us a user seat of our own. We never take a shared password or a login sheet.

We accept exports with no card or bank numbers, no Social Security or ID numbers, no health data and no GPS or location logs. We keep them encrypted and delete them once your sheet is delivered. If a breach ever touches them, we tell you within 10 days.

Do you record calls or film anything?

No. No video and no recorded calls. Florida law needs everyone's consent to record a call. The call history we use shows when calls came in and whether anyone answered, not what was said. Our fixes do not record or transcribe calls either. Some answering tools record every call by default, so we leave them out.

Will this replace my office staff?

It replaces retyping, waiting and forgetting: the job typed into three places, the quote that waits until Friday, the follow-up nobody owns. What you do with the time it frees is up to you.

Do I have to switch software?

No, not by default. We build inside what you already pay for when it can do the job. If a fix needs a new subscription, you hear the monthly cost before we start, and why the free option lost.

Do you sell software?

No, we don't sell software. We set up and connect the tools you already have, and when nothing on the market fits how your shop works, we build a custom tool for it, often in Python, that runs in your own accounts.

Most fixes are a setting, a spreadsheet or a small tool inside what you already use. Where a fix uses AI, it is on your own account and only on what you approve. We take no commissions on the tools we suggest.

Can you text my customers for me?

Your business sends the texts, from your own number, and you approve every word. We set them up to follow written rules: a missed-call text says sorry, when you will call back and how to reply or stop, and nothing else; job texts need the customer's OK at booking; anything that sells needs written consent, a written do-not-text policy and a check with the state on its telemarketing license; one STOP ends every text.

On a client's line, the owner approves its exact words in writing before it runs. Our article has the five rules in plain words. This is general information, not legal advice.

What if the fix does not work?

Every fix is built to fall back to how your shop worked before, and to alert us when it does. We look at it the next business morning, and in storm season you can add a same-evening check.

The monthly check shows what the fix did in your own numbers. If it did not move them, you see that there, and you can cancel any month.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel before the visit starts and before your call history is shared, and you get every dollar back. After that, if the sheet wasn't worth it to you, text or email us within 7 days of the call where you go over it, and you get every dollar back.

You keep the sheet either way. The monthly check runs month to month: cancel by email before the next billing date. If you stop a fix or a build partway, the agreement says what is owed for the work done.

Do you work in Spanish?

Yes. Spanish is a first language here, not a translation. The free call, the walk, your sheet, the monthly check and the one-page handover sheet can all be in Spanish. If your booking tool texts customers only in English, that can be one of the leaks we fix.
