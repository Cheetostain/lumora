1. The free conversation

01

It takes 20 minutes and costs nothing. We bring one number about your shop, taken from its public listings, like the hours it shows or how long since the last review.

“Your listing says you close at five. The sheet will show what happens to the calls after that.”

You tell us what eats your week. We tell you plainly whether a walk fits your shop.

2. The walk

02

The walk: $500, flat. Counts toward the fix if it starts within 30 days.

Paid when booked. It has three parts.

The visit
In week one, sized to your shop. We look at the phone, the whiteboard, the trucks and the invoice pile, and follow a job from the first call to the paid invoice.
The call data
With your written OK, the past 30 days from the call history your phone company already keeps. Where it keeps none, we collect the next 30 days. The history shows when calls came in and whether anyone answered. It is not a recording.
The sheet
Within five business days of having the data, on a walkthrough call: every leak with a dollar figure, the formula behind it and the effort to fix it. If a leak can be closed with a setting you already pay for, the sheet says so.

The sheet is yours to keep, whether or not you hire us for a fix.

What we need from you

  • Your written OK for the call history
  • Exports of your quotes and invoices, which you run, or a user seat of our own
  • Your calendar
  • A time for the visit, and one for the walkthrough call

Never a shared password. How we handle your records is on the privacy page.

3. One fix

03

Priced from the sheet

We start with the leak that recovers the most for the effort, one you can watch move in your own records within 90 days. Then we fix it inside what you already use: your phone line, Excel, QuickBooks, Jobber, a group chat.

The fix is a fixed price, never by the hour. You hear it with the sheet in hand, before any work starts. Any account the fix needs is opened in your name, and its cost shows on its own line.

If a fix ever breaks, it falls back to how your shop worked before, and we get an alert. We look at it the next business morning.

4. The monthly check

04

Sized to the shop

One page, once a month: what the fix did in your numbers, what broke and how we handled it, the next leak with a dollar figure and how many change requests your plan includes.

Cancel any month, by email. In storm season you can add a same-evening check on the after-hours flow, quoted from the sheet like everything else.

A full build

If you would rather do the whole shop at once, that is a full build, quoted from the sheet too. We suggest one fix first, to see it hold.

A full sample sheet

This is what you get after the walk, with made-up numbers for a 2-truck plumbing shop. Every priced row shows its formula, so you can check the math.

What sets the price after the walk

Everything after the walk is quoted from your sheet, so the number comes after the walk, not before. Three things move it:

  • How big the leak is
  • How many trucks (or locations) you run
  • What your tools can already do

That is why there is no price list. Two shops in the same trade can leak different amounts, in different places. The walk is the one fixed price.

Questions owners ask

What does it cost?

The walk: $500, flat. Counts toward the fix if it starts within 30 days. It is paid when booked, and the sheet is yours to keep even if you never hire us for a fix. Everything after the walk is quoted from your sheet: the fix is a fixed price, never by the hour, and the monthly check is sized to your shop. You hear each price before any work starts. The first conversation is free.

What happens in the free conversation?

It takes 20 minutes. We bring one number about your shop from its public listings, like the hours it shows or how long since your last review. You tell us what eats your week. We tell you plainly whether a walk fits.

Do you work outside Gainesville?

We are based in Gainesville and walk shops across Alachua County. Farther in Florida by arrangement. Every walk includes a visit, so call and we will tell you if the drive works.

What data do you need, and what do you do with it?

Three things from your own records: the call history your phone company keeps, your quote and invoice exports and your calendar. We use the call history only with your written OK. You run the exports, or give us a user seat of our own. We never take a shared password or a login sheet. We accept exports with no card or bank numbers, no Social Security or ID numbers, no health data and no GPS or location logs. We keep them encrypted and delete them once your sheet is delivered.

Do you record calls or put in cameras?

No. No cameras. No recorded calls. Florida law needs everyone’s consent to record a call. The call history we use shows when calls came in and whether anyone answered, not what was said. Our fixes do not record or transcribe calls either. Some answering tools record every call by default, so we leave them out.

Will this replace my office staff?

It replaces retyping, waiting and forgetting: the job typed into three places, the quote that waits until Friday, the follow-up nobody owns. What you do with the time it frees is up to you.

Do I have to switch software?

No, not by default. We build inside what you already pay for when it can do the job. If a fix needs a new subscription, you hear the monthly cost before we start, and why the free option lost.

Do you sell software?

No. We sell operations work: finding what your office leaks and fixing it. We set up and connect the tools you already have, and write small pieces of code only where no tool does the job. Some fixes use AI. Most are a phone rule or a spreadsheet. We take no commissions on the tools we suggest.

Can you text my customers for me?

Your business sends the texts, from your own number, and you approve every word. We set them up to follow written rules: a missed-call text says sorry, when you will call back and how to reply or stop, and nothing else; job texts need the customer’s OK at booking; anything that sells needs written consent; one STOP ends every text. No missed-call text runs on a client’s line until a Florida attorney has reviewed the script. Our article has the five rules in plain words. This is general information, not legal advice.

What if the fix does not work?

Every fix is built to fall back to how your shop worked before, and to alert us when it does. We look at it the next business morning, and in storm season you can add a same-evening check. The monthly check shows what the fix did in your own numbers. If it did not move them, you see that there, and you can cancel any month.

Can I cancel?

Yes. The monthly check runs month to month: cancel by email before the next billing date. The walk is paid when booked, and the sheet is yours either way. If you stop a fix or a build partway, the agreement says what is owed for the work done.

Do you work in Spanish?

Yes. Spanish is a first language here, not a translation. The conversation, the walk, your sheet, the monthly check and the one-page handover sheet can all be in Spanish. If your booking tool texts customers only in English, that can be one of the leaks we fix.
