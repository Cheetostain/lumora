1Step 1: The free call

Free Up to 20 minutes, by phone.

Before we talk, we look your shop up on Google, so you don't spend the call explaining what you do.

We bring one number from it, like how many reviews it shows. If your listing shows 12 reviews after years of jobs, we ask who sends the review request when a job is done. If nobody does, that is office work a fix can take over.

You tell us what eats your week. We tell you plainly whether a walk fits.

If someone else keeps your books, bring them on the call. If you say no to a walk, we don't call or text again unless you ask.

2Step 2: The walk

Paid Up front, when you book.

It has three parts: the visit, your records and the sheet.

The walk starts at $500. Bigger shops get a quote on the free call. Counts toward a fix of $1,500 or more that starts within 30 days.

Not worth it to you? Text or email us within 7 days of the call where you go over your sheet, and you get the walk fee back.

Your records

Invoices, calendar, job list and call history. You run the exports, or give us a user seat of our own. The call history comes from your phone company, with your written OK; where it keeps none, your staff keeps a tally by the phone for 30 days. It shows when calls came in and whether anyone answered, not what was said.

The sheet

On a walkthrough call: every problem with a dollar figure, the formula behind it and the effort to fix it. If a setting you already pay for fixes one, the sheet says so.

The sheet is yours to keep, whether or not you hire us for a fix.

What we need from you

  • Your written OK for the call history
  • Exports of your quotes, invoices and calendar, which you run, or a user seat of our own
  • Your profit on a typical job and your office's hourly wage, from your books
  • A time for the visit, and one for the walkthrough call

Never a shared password. How we handle your records is on the privacy page.

What the walk never does

  • Renew, or add a charge. It's one charge.
  • Ask for a password.
  • Change anything in your phone, software or accounts.
  • Text your customers.
  • Put your files into an AI tool unless you say yes. They're deleted once your sheet is delivered.

Where the sample numbers come from

$341 is HomeAdvisor's national average plumber visit (2026, cost guide); 44.3% is Level's median job gross margin (vendor data). 24% is the share of calls that shops with fewer than 5 technicians booked at all hours (ServiceTitan blog, 2022, vendor data).

On the AC and heating sheet, 1 in 4 comes from ServiceTitan's January 2026 data on commercial service agreements: 29.8% renewed when renewal was manual and 49.1% with auto-renew and email reminders, so about 1 in 4 of the plans that would have lapsed were kept (vendor data). $189 is a sample yearly fee for a plan with two tune-ups, within published Florida plan prices.

On the property manager sheet, 10% is a common maintenance markup in management agreements (BiggerPockets forum, owners and managers reporting; 0 to 20% seen, or a flat fee instead). 70 bills is a sample: about 5% of the 1,400 or so vendor bills a 212-door office pays in a year (about 600 in turn week, plus 3 to 5 work orders a door), at a sample $180 a bill.

On the pest control sheet, 1 in 2 and 20% are our own sample rates, and your walk puts your own numbers in their place: 1 in 2 lapsed termite renewals won back by a follow-up call, and 20% more declined autopay charges recovered once retries run on a schedule. For scale, Recurly's 2018 study of more than 1,300 subscription businesses found that over 45% of the charges declined for the three most common reasons were recovered (vendor data). $225 is a renewal's $271 average fee across $325 bait and $195 liquid agreements, less a $46 yearly inspection. On the pest control page, $7.50 a foot for trench-and-rod termite treatment sits inside two national cost guides, Fixr's $4 to $14.50 and Polyguard's $3 to $15.

4Step 4: One fix

Paid Only if you want it.

We suggest starting with one fix you can watch move in your own records within 90 days.

Each fix has its own fixed price, never by the hour, and you hear it with the sheet in hand, before any work starts. You pay half when you sign and half when it's working. Any software it needs is opened in your name, at cost, never marked up.

Want everything fixed at once? We quote that from the sheet too, and the prices add up.

Fixed rules you approve, AI, or both: your call. Anything AI writes waits for your OK, and nothing goes into an AI tool without it.

Everything runs in accounts you own. If a fix breaks, it falls back to how your shop worked before, and we look at it the next business morning.

What a fix can look like

  • Every supplier bill checked against the job it was bought for.
  • Finished jobs listed each morning until each one is invoiced.
  • The quote sent the day of the visit, with a reminder to you if nobody answers.
  • A job typed once and filled in everywhere else.
  • Emails and attachments read and filed into the right job folder.
  • Reminders to your crew and customers, all year.

The monthly check

Paid Month to month, once a fix is working.

One page, once a month: what the fix did in your numbers, what broke and how we handled it, the next problem with a dollar figure, and up to 2 small changes a month.

At 90 days we compare what the fix brought back with what the sheet said. If it brought back less, the monthly check goes down to match.

The price for the fixes you have does not go up in the first year. After that we review it once a year, and it goes up only if our work for you has grown, never because your shop got busier, with 30 days' notice. A fix you add later brings its own part of the price, shown before you sign. Cancel any month, by email.

What the monthly check covers, and what it doesn't

Covered

  • Watching every fix we built
  • Fixing what breaks, starting the next business morning, even when another company changes its software
  • Reconnecting logins
  • Up to 2 small changes a month, each about an hour of work or less
  • Alerts before a software plan limit stops a fix, where the software offers them
  • The one-page report
  • The next problem, with a dollar figure

Not covered

  • New fixes, each quoted on its own
  • Copying a fix to a new location or moving it to new software, each quoted on its own
  • Rebuilding a fix after another company removes something it needs, quoted with the options
  • Software subscriptions, in your name and at cost
  • Hardware
  • Time when another company's software is down
  • Marketing or ads
  • Dispatch software

Questions owners ask

What does it cost?

The walk's price is printed with step 2 above, and it is paid when booked. You keep the sheet either way, even if you never hire us for a fix.

Everything after the walk is quoted from your sheet, so that number comes after the walk, not before. Three things move it: how big the problem is, how many trucks or locations you run, and what your tools can already do. That is why there is no price list. Two shops in the same trade can lose different amounts, in different places.

Each fix has its own fixed price, never by the hour, so you can start with one. Any software fees show on their own line, at cost. The monthly check is sized to your shop, and if a fix brings back less than the sheet said, it costs less. You hear each price before any work starts. The first conversation is free.

Do you work outside Gainesville?

We are based in Gainesville and walk shops across Alachua County. Farther in Florida by arrangement. Every walk includes a visit, so call and we will tell you if the drive works.

What data do you need, and what do you do with it?

Your own records: invoices, quotes, your calendar and job list, and the call history your phone company keeps. We use the call history only with your written OK. You run the exports, or give us a user seat of our own. We never take a shared password or a login sheet.

We accept exports with no card or bank numbers, no Social Security or ID numbers, no health data and no GPS or location logs. We keep them encrypted and delete them once your sheet is delivered. If a breach ever touches them, we tell you within 10 days.

Do you record calls or film anything?

Only with your OK. Florida law also needs everyone on a call to agree before it is recorded. If you would rather nothing is photographed or recorded, that is the rule. The call history we use shows when calls came in and whether anyone answered, not what was said. Some answering tools record every call by default, so we keep that switched off unless you want it and every caller agrees first.

Will this replace my office staff?

It replaces the retyping and the chasing, like the job typed into three places or the quote that waits until Friday. What you do with the time it frees is up to you.

Do I have to switch software?

No, not by default. We build inside what you already pay for when it can do the job. If a fix needs a new subscription, you hear the monthly cost before we start, and why the free option lost. If you switch software later, moving your fixes is quoted on its own, and we tell you first what the new software can and can't do.

What happens when my shop grows or I change software?

Tell us first. Within 2 business days we tell you in writing what it touches and what it would cost, before you sign up for anything. More jobs, trucks or staff on the fixes you have cost nothing extra from us.

A new location or new software means copying or moving fixes, and each one is quoted on its own before any work. Your software fees grow with your shop and stay in your name.

Do you sell software?

No, we don't sell software. We set up and connect the tools you already have, and when nothing on the market fits how your shop works, we build a custom tool for it, often in Python, that runs in your own accounts.

Most fixes are a setting, a spreadsheet or a small tool inside what you already use. Where a fix uses AI, it is on your own account and only on what you approve. If you'd rather nothing leave your office, we can run it on a computer at your shop instead. That costs extra to set up and adds to the monthly check, and we quote it with the fix. We take no commissions on the tools we suggest.

Can you text my customers for me?

Your business sends the texts, from your own number, and you approve every word. We set them up to follow written rules: a missed-call text says sorry, when you will call back and how to reply or stop, and nothing else; job texts need the customer's OK at booking; anything that sells needs written consent, a written do-not-text policy and a check with the state on its telemarketing license; one STOP ends every text.

On a client's line, the owner approves its exact words in writing before it runs. Our article has the five rules in plain words.

What if the fix does not work?

Every fix is built to fall back to how your shop worked before, and to alert us when it does. We look at it the next business morning, and in storm season you can add a same-evening check.

The monthly check shows what the fix did in your own numbers. If it did not move them, you see that there, and you can cancel any month.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel before the visit starts and before your call history is shared, and you get the walk fee back. After that, if the sheet wasn't worth it to you, text or email us within 7 days of the call where you go over it, and you get the walk fee back.

You keep the sheet either way. The monthly check runs month to month: cancel by email before the next billing date. If you stop a fix or a build partway, the agreement says what is owed for the work done.

Do you work in Spanish?

Yes. The free call, the walk, your sheet, the monthly check and the one-page handover sheet can all be in Spanish. If your booking tool texts customers only in English, that can be one of the problems we fix.
