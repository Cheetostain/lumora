The short version

  • This site has no forms, no ads, no cookies and no analytics.
  • The leak check runs on your device. Nothing you type is sent to us or saved.
  • We only get what you choose to send us: a call, a text or an email.
  • On a walk, we take only exports you run, keep them encrypted and delete them once your sheet is delivered.
  • We do not sell your information or share it for marketing.

What this site collects

Nothing beyond what you send us. There are no forms, no accounts, no ads, no cookies and no analytics.

The site remembers one thing in your browser: whether you chose English or Spanish. That setting stays on your device. Clearing your browser’s data for this site removes it.

The fonts and images load from this site, not from other companies.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical details such as IP addresses to run and protect its service. GitHub’s own privacy statement covers those logs. We add no tracking of our own.

The leak check does its math on your phone or computer. Nothing you type is sent to us or saved.

Some pages link to other sites, such as the sources in our articles. Those sites have their own privacy rules.

This site is for business owners. It is not meant for children.

When you call, text or email us

We get your phone number or email address and whatever you tell us. We use it to answer you and to set up the conversation. We do not add you to a mailing list, and we do not send marketing texts.

We do not record calls.

Emails are kept by our email provider.

What you share on a walk, and how we handle it

A walk runs on your own records. Here is how we treat them.

  • Call data comes from the call history your phone company keeps, with your written OK.
  • You run the exports, or give us a user seat of our own. We never take a shared password or a login sheet.
  • We accept exports with no card or bank numbers, no Social Security or ID numbers, no health data and no GPS or location logs. If one arrives with them, we delete it and ask for a clean one.
  • We keep exports encrypted, and delete them once your sheet is delivered.
  • No cameras. No recorded calls.

Fixes run in your own accounts, in your name. We keep no copies of your passwords.

If a fix or a report uses an outside AI service, our agreement names the service, says it does not train on your data and says how long it keeps data.

Deleting your data

Walk exports are deleted once your sheet is delivered.

You can ask us to delete your messages and contact details at any time. Call, text or email us. We keep only what we must: the record of a STOP request, so we never text you again, and records the law or your agreement requires.

Changes

If this notice changes, we update this page and the date at the top.
