Lumora icons: Material Symbols by Google

The icons in this folder are Google's Material Symbols, Sharp, weight 300 (the red drop is water_drop, filled),
from the npm package @material-symbols/svg-300 version 0.47.5 and from Google's repository
https://github.com/google/material-design-icons. They are used under the Apache License, Version 2.0, a copy of
which is in LICENSE.txt beside this file.

Changed by Lumora Group LLC: each glyph is placed on a 48- or 24-unit box and colored, and each of the six leak
icons adds the water_drop glyph in red at its lower right. The glyph outlines are Google's, unchanged.
