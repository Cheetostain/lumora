Why Lumora

The person who walks your shop goes over your sheet with you, quotes the fix and does the monthly check. Every page you get ends with the same phone number.

The work is operations

We start with the computer work, because that is where the hours go. When it clearly pays, the same approach reaches the stockroom and the equipment, one fix at a time, in accounts you own, and only with your OK.

First we follow one job through your shop, from the first call to the paid invoice. The fix comes from what that shows, and only then do we suggest a tool.

Where the hours go

  • Typing the same job twice.
  • Jobs finished but never billed.
  • Supplier bills that don't match the quote.
  • Reading emails and filing the attachments by hand.
  • Reminding crew and customers, one at a time.
  • Adding up the month-end numbers.

On the walk, we find which ones your shop has. See the full list

We do not sell software, and we take no commissions on the tools we suggest.

Rules we keep

  1. Every fix goes after a task you can name. It might be the job typed into three places, or the quote that waits until Friday.

  2. We build inside what you already pay for. If a fix needs a new subscription, you hear the monthly cost before we start, and why the free option lost.

  3. No filming or recording without your OK. Photos only of what you point to, unless we agree in writing on more. We work from your call history, invoices and calendar, once you sign the OK to look at them.

  4. Cancel the monthly check any month. One email before the next billing date is enough.

  5. Say no once, and that's the end of it. After a no, we don't call, text or stop by again unless you ask.

  6. Every number says where it came from. Your sheet is built from your own records. Samples say Sample.
