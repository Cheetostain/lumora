Why Lumora

We walk your business, go over your sheet with you, quote and build the fix, and do the monthly check. One Gainesville number for all of it.

We start with the office work

The computer work comes first, because that is where the hours go. Then the same approach reaches the rest of the business, from the stockroom to the equipment, in accounts you own.

We look at how the work moves through your business first, and only then suggest a tool.

Where the hours go

  • Typing the same names and numbers twice.
  • Work finished but never billed.
  • Supplier bills that don't match what you ordered.
  • Reading emails and filing the attachments by hand.
  • Reminding your team and customers, one at a time.
  • Adding up the month-end numbers.

On the walk, we find which ones your business has. See the full list

You never have to switch software, and we take no commissions on the tools we suggest.

Rules we keep

  1. Every fix goes after a task you can name. It might be the job typed into three places, or the quote that waits until Friday.

  2. We start with what you already pay for. If new software would pay for itself, you hear what it costs and what it should bring back, and you decide.

  3. No filming or recording without your OK. Photos only of what you point to.

  4. Cancel the monthly check any month. One email before the next billing date is enough.

  5. Say no once, and that's the end of it. After a no, we don't call, text or stop by again unless you ask.

  6. Your accounts and your data are yours from the start. What we build is yours, in writing, once it's paid. If we part, you keep all of it, with a one-page sheet in English or Spanish on running it.
