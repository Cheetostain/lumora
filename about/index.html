One contact, start to finish

The person who walks your shop is the person who reads your sheet with you, quotes the fix and checks it every month. If you call, that is who you reach.

The work is operations

Call it operations, or optimization. In plain words: we find where your office loses time and money, put a dollar figure on it and fix it, one leak at a time.

Software, automation and sometimes AI are tools we use. They are not what we sell. Most fixes are a phone rule or a spreadsheet, set up in the tools you already pay for.

We do not sell software, and we take no commissions on the tools we suggest.

Rules we keep

It replaces retyping, waiting and forgetting.

Every fix goes after a task: the job typed into three places, the quote that waits until Friday, the follow-up nobody owns.

  • We build inside what you already pay for. If a fix needs a new subscription, you hear the monthly cost before we start, and why the free option lost.

  • No cameras. No recorded calls. Florida law needs everyone’s consent to record a call, and a crew should not work under a camera. We work from your call history, your invoices and your calendar.

  • Cancel any month. The monthly check earns its place every month, or it ends.

  • No cold calls, no cold email. Work comes from people who pass our name along, and rooms we walk into.

  • Every number says where it came from. Your sheet is built from your own records. Samples say Sample.

In English or Spanish

Spanish is a first language here, not a translation. The conversation, the walk, your sheet, the monthly check and the one-page handover sheet can all be in Spanish.

We offer it and never assume it. You choose the language, and we keep to it.

Built on our own work first

We built these systems for our own work before offering them to anyone. It starts by watching how your shop actually runs, not by selling software.

One bird

Our mark is one crested bird: the crest lifts off the crown, and the body sweeps into the tail.

We call it Cardino, in English and in Spanish.
