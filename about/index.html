One contact, start to finish. The person who walks your shop is the person who reads your sheet with you, quotes the fix and checks it every month. If you call, that is who you reach.

The work is operations

Call it operations, or optimization. In plain words: we follow a job from the first call to the next visit, find where your office loses time and money, put a dollar figure on it and fix it, one leak at a time.

Before we suggest any tool, we follow one job through your shop, from the first call to the paid invoice. The fix comes from what that shows, not from software we want to sell.

Software, automation and sometimes AI are tools we use. They are not what we sell. Most fixes are a setting, a spreadsheet or a small tool inside what you already pay for.

We do not sell software, and we take no commissions on the tools we suggest.

In writing, so you can hold us to them.

Rules we keep

  1. It replaces retyping, waiting and forgetting. Every fix goes after a task: the job typed into three places, the quote that waits until Friday, the follow-up nobody owns.

  2. We build inside what you already pay for. If a fix needs a new subscription, you hear the monthly cost before we start, and why the free option lost.

  3. No video. No recorded calls. Florida law needs everyone's consent to record a call, and nobody's crew should work under a camera. A photo only of a board or a process you point to, never of your people or your customers' papers. We work from your call history, your invoices and your calendar.

  4. Cancel any month. The monthly check earns its place every month, or it ends.

  5. Say no once, and that's the end of it. After a no, we don't call, text or stop by again unless you ask.

  6. Every number says where it came from. Your sheet is built from your own records. Samples say Sample.
