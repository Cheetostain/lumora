What happens after you reach out

  1. Today

    Free

    A free call, up to 20 minutes.

  2. The walk

    Paid

    A visit sized to your shop, plus your records: invoices, calendar, job list and call history. You sign one OK so we can look at them.

    The walk starts at $500. Bigger shops get a quote on the free call. Counts toward a fix of $1,500 or more that starts within 30 days.

    Not worth it to you? Text or email us within 7 days of the call where you go over your sheet, and you get the walk fee back. Full terms

  3. Your sheet

    Every problem in dollars. About 5 business days after we have your records; bigger shops get a quote and a date on the free call. You keep it either way.

  4. Then, only if you want

    Paid

    One fix, priced before work starts, and a monthly check you can cancel any month.
