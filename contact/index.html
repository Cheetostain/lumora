What happens after you reach out. You can stop after any step.

  1. 1We set a time

    We call or text back to set a time for the free call.

  2. 2The free call, up to 20 minutes

    Before we talk, we look your shop up on Google, so you don't spend the call explaining what you do. You tell us what eats your week, and we tell you plainly whether a walk fits. If someone else keeps your books, bring them on the call. If you say no, we don't call or text again unless you ask.

  3. 3If you book, the walk

    The walk is paid when booked. The walk: $500, flat. Counts toward the fix if it starts within 30 days. Not worth it to you? Text or email us within 7 days of the call where you go over your sheet, and you get every dollar back. You keep the sheet.

  4. 4Week one

    We visit, and you sign the OK for your call history. The sheet comes within five business days of having the data, on a walkthrough call.

  5. 5Then you decide

    The sheet is yours either way.
