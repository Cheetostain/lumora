What happens after you reach out

  1. Today

    Free

    A free call, by phone.

  2. The walk

    Paid

    A visit to your business, and a look at your records.

    The walk starts at $500. We set your price on the free call, by the size of your business, and it doesn't change. Start a fix of $1,500 or more within 30 days of going over your sheet and the walk fee comes off its price.

    Not worth it to you? Text or email us within 7 days of the call where you go over your sheet, and you get the walk fee back. No reason needed, and you keep the sheet. Full terms

  3. Your sheet

    What you lose each month, in dollars, with the math shown, on the schedule we set with you on the free call. We go over it with you by phone and give you a fixed price for each fix you want, never by the hour. You keep it either way.

  4. Then, if you want

    Paid

    Pick one problem and we fix it, most often by automating the work. The fix is yours once it's paid. An optional monthly check keeps it working and shows what it brought back. Cancel any month.
