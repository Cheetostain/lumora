What we automate in a property management office

It all lands in what you already use: AppFolio, Buildium, Excel or a simple form we build.

  • Tenant repair requests sorted by unit and urgency, each work order carrying the owner's spending limit.

The damage ran past the deposit, and the guarantor never got the bill.

On a sample student lease, the claim notice went out certified on day 21 and the deposit was applied. Within two miles of UF, the average deposit with a guarantor is $52, about 4% of the average without one (UF Off-Campus Life, 2026-27).

The fix: every move-out with charges past the deposit goes on one list with its guarantor, and the bill is drafted for your OK. On your walk, we count last August's unbilled balances.

Your office writes the claim notice and mails it certified, or emails it only where you and the tenant signed the email addendum (F.S. 83.49 and 83.505).
