What we automate in a property management office

Most fixes land in what you already use: AppFolio, Buildium, Excel or a simple form we build.

  • Tenant repair requests sorted by unit and urgency, each work order carrying the owner's spending limit.
  • Subcontractor and vendor insurance certificates read for their expiry dates, with a warning before one lapses.

The damage ran past the deposit, and the guarantor never got the bill.

On a sample student lease, the claim notice went out certified on day 21 and the deposit was applied. Within two miles of UF, the average deposit with a guarantor is $52, about 4% of the average without one (UF Off-Campus Life, 2026-27). With a deposit that small, almost any damage runs past it, and the guarantor owes the rest once your office sends the bill.

The fix: every move-out with charges past the deposit goes on one list with its guarantor, and the bill is drafted for your OK. This is one of the things a walk looks for.

Your office writes the claim notice and mails it certified, or emails it only where you and the tenant signed the email addendum (F.S. 83.49 and 83.505).
