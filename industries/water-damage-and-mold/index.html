Claim 0026-1093-3H, a sample job. Two weeks after the file went out, the adjuster asks for the dry log and the signed certificate of completion. One is on Luis's phone, the other in the truck. The carrier's $1,709.90 waits. Florida gives the insurer 60 days from notice to pay or deny (F.S. 627.70131), and this file is already 21 days into them.

The fix: each file checked before it goes out against what adjusters ask for, and anything missing sent to you while the tech has it. Your office sends the file. Nothing goes to the insurer from us, and we never talk coverage.
