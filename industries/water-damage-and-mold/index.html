Rules for your trade

The rules under your numbers

  1. The homeowner owes the deductible. On policies issued since January 1, 2023, a homeowner can't sign the claim over to you, so the carrier's check comes in short by the deductible and someone has to bill the rest (F.S. 627.7152). Stop 2 counts the jobs where nobody did.

  2. No gifts for an inspection or a claim. No text, box or promotion offers a homeowner a rebate, gift card, cash, coupon, deductible waiver or any other thing of value for a roof inspection or an insurance claim, and no automation pays a referral reward on a job funded by insurance proceeds (F.S. 489.147, up to $10,000 a violation).

  3. Assessor and remediator stay separate. No automation routes paid leads between a mold assessor and a remediator (F.S. 468.8419). The clearance test on a job you remediated goes to a separate licensed assessor, never to your own crew.

General information, not legal advice.
