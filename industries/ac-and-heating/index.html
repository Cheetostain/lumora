What we automate in an AC and heating shop

It all lands in what you already use: Excel, QuickBooks, your job app or a simple form we build.

  • Home warranty dispatches and builder work orders, read from the emailed PDF into your job list.
  • A photo of the unit's label at booking, read into the job, so the right part rides on the truck.
  • Refrigerant pounds in the tech's notes, checked against the invoice for your OK.
  • Warranty parts matched to the supply house's credit, so you don't pay full price for a covered part.
  • Financed change-outs on one list until the lender pays, with a nudge to you when the customer hasn't signed off.
  • Change-out permits checked on Citizenserve and PermitGNV until the final inspection passes.
