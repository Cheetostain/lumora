What we automate in any business

Most fixes land in what you already use: Excel, QuickBooks, your booking app or a tool we build.

  • A first reply drafted in English or Spanish, whichever the customer wrote in, ready for you to send.
  • Attachments read into your own tools: insurer PDFs, Word files, spreadsheets and scanned paper.
  • The same supplier bill entered twice, or a return with no credit, caught before you pay.
  • One page every morning: what came in, what got done and what needs your OK.
  • Reminders for the whole year on one calendar: license and insurance renewals, tax dates, certificates and inspections.
  • Logins removed when someone leaves, and emails asking to change bank details flagged before anyone pays.
