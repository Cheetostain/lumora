Day 28 of 30, and no permit application on file.

Job TR-26-133, a sample retail reroof. Nancy Allen paid a 30 percent deposit on Wednesday, September 9: $4,563.00 on a $15,210.00 contract. Over 10 percent down, the permit application is due within 30 days of the payment, unless she agreed in writing to a longer time (F.S. 489.126). On day 28 the county portal shows none. The estimator meant to file it between jobs.

The fix: a deposit over 10 percent starts a clock on your job list, with a reminder to you on day 20 and the permit details ready for your office to file. Your walk counts the unfiled jobs past day 20.
