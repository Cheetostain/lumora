The report found termites. No quote went out.

Job WDO-26-0231, a sample home-sale inspection. The state form shows mud tubes on the foundation and damage at the garage door. The inspection is paid for, but nobody started the termite quote, and the inspection period ends Friday, October 9. At $7.50 a foot for 196 feet of trench and rod, that's a $1,470 job, with a renewal every year after it.

The fix: every report that finds evidence or damage starts a quote draft the same day, for your OK, before the inspection period ends. The report stays your inspector's, on the state form with nothing added. On your walk, we count how many reports went out like this.
