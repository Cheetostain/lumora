The report found termites. No quote went out.

Job WDO-26-0231, a sample home-sale inspection. The state form shows mud tubes on the foundation and damage at the garage door. The inspection is paid for, but the termite quote hasn't been started, and the inspection period ends Friday, October 9. At $7.50 a foot for 196 feet of trench and rod, that's a $1,470 job, with a renewal every year after it.

The fix: every report that finds evidence or damage starts a quote draft the same day, for your OK, before the inspection period ends. The report stays your inspector's, on the state form with nothing added. This is one of the things a walk looks for.
