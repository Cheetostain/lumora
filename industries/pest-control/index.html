Mud tubes on the foundation. Report sent to the agent. No quote ever went out.

On most home sales the seller pays for treatment up to the repair limit in the contract, and the report already says what was found and where. The quote was the easy part.

Also on your sheet

Two more lines we count, and the rules we keep

  1. Calls that ring out on route days. When every tech is out on route, the office phone rings out. The sheet counts the calls that were never returned, day and night.

  2. A renewal paid is an inspection owed. Florida requires a signed report for every annual termite inspection, so a renewal paid with no inspection on file is still owed. The sheet counts the inspections not done.

  3. Review asks follow written rules. Thanks and one link, daytime only, no coupon, and every customer who said texting was fine gets asked, not only the happy ones. The wording is checked against the FTC's review rules before the first one goes out.

General information, not legal advice.
