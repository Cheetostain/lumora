Panel jobs

Panel jobs waiting on three dates. When they live in someone's head, the crew waits and the balance waits with it.

A service change needs a GRU disconnect booked about a week or more ahead, the county or city inspection, and a reconnect GRU makes only after the inspector approves (GRU service guide). In parts of the county the utility is Clay Electric or Duke instead.
