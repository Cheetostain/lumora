What we automate in an electrical shop

Most fixes land in what you already use: Excel, QuickBooks, your job app or a simple form we build.

  • Property manager and builder work orders, read from the emailed PDF into your job list.

Panel jobs waiting on three dates.

In GRU's area, a service change needs a disconnect booked ahead, the inspection, and GRU's reconnect once the inspector approves. Your list tracks all three and drafts the balance invoice when the power is back on.

Before The GRU call waits for someone to remember, and the balance waits until someone hears the inspection passed.

Outside GRU's area, the list tracks the same three dates with whichever utility serves the job.
