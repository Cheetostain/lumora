What we automate in a restaurant or bar

It all lands in what you already use: Excel, QuickBooks, your bookkeeper's sheet or a simple form we build.

  • Distributor invoices, photographed or emailed as PDFs, read line by line into your books.
  • Last night's close checked against the bank deposit, with voids and comps listed by who approved them, for you only.
  • Tip-outs worked out from each server's sales the way your pool rule says, ready before payroll.
  • Sunday's inventory count typed once from the clipboard, next to what it should have been.
  • Each app's payout matched to the bank deposit, with its real cut after promotions and ads.
  • Food handler training due 60 days after each hire, license renewals and grease trap pump-outs, on one calendar.

A case of fries on the invoice never came off the truck.

Monday's delivery, a sample. Coastway's invoice bills 10 cases of fries. Nine came in. The cook wrote the 9 in pencil after the driver left, so the driver's copy says nothing, and nobody asked for the credit.

The fix: the count at the back door goes into a photo or a short form, each invoice is checked against it, and a credit request is drafted for your OK by the next morning. On your walk, we check your marked copies for shorts with no credit.
