Where the first fix will likely start

A check that reads each supplier invoice once. It flags any price that changed, and any case that never came off the truck.

Often it reads each supplier invoice into QuickBooks once and flags any price that moved, with a monthly look at staff hours against sales. If a texting list for slow nights makes sense, it is built only from guests who signed up in writing.

The sheet decides, not this page. We pick the leak that recovers the most for the effort, one you can watch move in your own records within 90 days. The price after the walk depends on how big the leak is, how many locations you run and what your point-of-sale system can already do.

Heat , storms , Gator games and the school calendar all move a dining room.
