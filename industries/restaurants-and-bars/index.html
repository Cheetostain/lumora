01

What the walk looks for

  • Supplier invoices typed by hand

    Every delivery comes with an invoice, and someone types it into QuickBooks. Prices creep and short deliveries slip through. The sheet times the typing and checks a month of your invoices against the prices you agreed.

  • Staff hours that run past the sales

    Shifts get scheduled on a guess, and overtime shows up at payroll. The sheet lines up your clock-ins with your sales by hour, from your own reports.

  • Food cost and delivery fees

    Supplier prices move while menu prices stand still, and each delivery app keeps its cut. The sheet checks your top items’ cost against their price, and what each app really keeps from your payout reports.

  • Reviews nobody answers

    In a 2026 survey of 1,002 US adults, 80 percent said they are likelier to use a business that answers every review (BrightLocal, 2026, vendor survey). The sheet counts the reviews nobody answered.
