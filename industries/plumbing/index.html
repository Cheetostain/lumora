What we automate in a plumbing shop

Most fixes land in what you already use: Excel, QuickBooks, your job app or a tool we build.

  • Property manager and home-warranty work orders, read from the emailed PDF into your job list.
  • Jobs waiting on a part, booked the day the supply house says it's in.
  • Finished jobs listed every morning until each one is invoiced.
  • Supplier bills checked for returns with no credit and prices above what you agreed.
  • Water heater permits on your list the next business day after an emergency swap, and checked on PermitGNV and Citizenserve until inspection.
  • Reminders all year to your customers, like water heater flushes and yearly backflow tests.

Night jobs billed at the after-hours rate, with the permit on the morning list.

If you take night jobs, the line forwards to whoever is on call that week, with a card in English and Spanish. The next workday, the invoice has the after-hours line and the permit is on your list.

Before Every night call lands on your cell, the job is billed at the day rate, and Monday's permit waits for someone to remember.

Every water heater swap needs a permit, and after a weekend emergency swap Florida's building code lets the application go in the next business day (FBC 105.2.1). A City of Gainesville permit expires if no inspection is requested within 180 days of being issued.
