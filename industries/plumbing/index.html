Where the first fix will likely start

Often at the invoice or at the phone .

The invoice: a check that the parts and extras in the tech's notes reach the bill before it goes out. The phone: a rule on the line you already pay for, so calls that ring out go to a second phone or a callback list, with a missed-call text in words you approve. Either one shows in your own records the next month.

The sheet decides, not this page. We pick the leak that recovers the most for the effort, one you can watch move in your own records within 90 days.

The season. Heat, storms and UF move-in weeks swing the call volume, so we count from your call history, not from one day on site.
